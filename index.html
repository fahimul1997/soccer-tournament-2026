📋 Tournament Rules

These are the tournament rules currently implemented on the website. Players and team officials should review them before participating.

1. Tournament Format

  • Forfeit: If a team forfeits a game, the opposing team is awarded a 3–0 win. The administrator may enter the three goals without assigning a scorer.
  • 8 teams participate, divided into 2 groups of 4: Group A and Group B.
  • Each team plays every other team in its group once.
  • Each team plays 3 group-stage games; each group has 6 games; 12 group-stage games are played in total.
  • The top 2 teams from each group advance to the semifinals.
  • Semifinal 1: Group A #1 vs Group B #2.
  • Semifinal 2: Group B #1 vs Group A #2.
  • The semifinal winners advance to the final.

2. Game Format & Schedule

  • Games are 40 minutes long with a 5-minute halftime break.
  • Group A and Group B play simultaneously during the group stage, one game on each field at each time slot.
  • The two groups alternate fields so both Group A and Group B use both fields during the group stage.
  • Group-stage games begin at 10:00 AM, with 50-minute schedule slots: 40-minute game + 5-minute halftime + 5-minute changeover/buffer.
  • After all 12 group-stage matches, there will be a 30-minute lunch break. Lunch will be provided.
  • Semifinal 1 is scheduled for 3:20 PM on Field 1.
  • Semifinal 2 is scheduled for 4:05 PM on Field 2.
  • The final is scheduled for 5:25 PM on Field 1.

3. Standings & Points

  • Win = 3 points.
  • Draw = 1 point.
  • Loss = 0 points.
  • Standings are ordered by points, then goal difference, then goals scored.

4. Rosters & Player Eligibility

  • Maximum 15 players per team: No team may register more than 15 players for the tournament.
  • Goals and cards can be recorded only for players listed on a team roster.
  • Teams may add players during the group stage if their roster is not marked complete.
  • Once the group stage is locked, no new players can be added.
  • A player added after the group-stage deadline is not eligible for the knockout stage.
  • Player card history remains recorded and is not erased simply by changing roster entries.

5. Cards & Suspensions

  • Yellow cards are recorded against the player for the game in which they are received.
  • Two accumulated yellow cards result in a 1-game suspension.
  • A red card results in a 1-game suspension.
  • Two yellow cards in the same game result in a red-card dismissal.
  • A suspension is served in the player’s next eligible group-stage game or semifinal.
  • The final is exempt from suspension serving.
  • Card and suspension records are maintained in the tournament system.

6. FIFA Rules & Referee Decisions

  • FIFA Laws of the Game apply to tournament matches.
  • Shin guards are mandatory: Every player must wear shin guards during the game. Captains are responsible for ensuring their players wear shin guards and play cautiously, with player safety as the primary focus.
  • Referee decisions made during the game are final.
  • Players and team officials are expected to respect referee decisions and maintain safe, sportsmanlike play.
  • Disrespectful, abusive, or unsportsmanlike conduct toward opposing team players, other teams, referees, or tournament committee members may result in a 1-game suspension for the player’s next eligible game.

7. Match Recording

  • Game scores are recorded by the tournament administrator.
  • Goals are recorded with the game, team, player, and goal time.
  • Cards are recorded with the game, team, player, card type, and card time.
  • Only players from the teams participating in a game can be recorded for that game.
Player notice: By participating in the tournament, players and team officials should review these rules before their games. The website displays the rules currently implemented in the tournament system.
